# Monster-Energy-Sales-Performance-Dashboard
An interactive Monster Energy Sales Dashboard showing key metrics like Revenue, Profit, Units Sold, Cost, and Customer Rating. It also provides product-wise, country-wise, and sales-channel-wise insights, along with revenue trends and interactive filters for easy analysis.

This Power BI dashboard provides an interactive view of Monster Energy sales performance across different products, countries, and sales channels.

It includes key KPIs such as Total Revenue, Total Profit, Total Units Sold, Total Cost, and Average Customer Rating, giving a quick overview of overall business performance.

The dashboard also shows revenue trends over time, product-wise sales performance, and revenue distribution across different sales channels such as Online, Supermarket, Convenience Store, Distributor, Gym/Fitness, and Gas Station.

The country slicer allows users to filter the entire dashboard and explore performance for a specific country. The visualizations make it easier to compare products, identify sales patterns, and understand how different channels contribute to overall revenue.

The dashboard uses a dark theme with Monster Energy-inspired green highlights to keep the design consistent and visually engaging while keeping the information easy to read.
